Improving the Durability of Composite Skis

ME 440 UW · 2026

The Anisotropic Elasticity of Snow

Finite-element simulations of 391 micro-CT snow, firn and ice samples show that stiffness depends on microstructure direction, not just density. A model combining both predicts elasticity far more accurately (R² = 0.99) than density-only models.
